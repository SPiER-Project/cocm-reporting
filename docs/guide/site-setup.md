# Site setup

**Status:** not yet written.

The things a site settles once, before its first report, and revisits only when its
systems change. Planned checklist:

- Map every payer and plan to a [payer category](../reference/our-calls.md#who-counts-as-medicaid),
  including each Medicaid managed care plan by name.
- Record a [primary scale](../reference/our-calls.md#primary-scale) on every open episode,
  and choose PHQ-9 or SMFQ for adolescent depression.
- Put the [inactivity discharge](../reference/our-calls.md#inactivity-discharge) rule into
  the monthly workflow.
- Confirm the practice's [screening workflow](../reference/our-calls.md#who-should-be-screened)
  matches the guide's age floor and visit types, or disclose where it differs.
- Settle how the registry and the EHR will produce the same patient id.
- Attest [BHCM FTE](../reference/our-calls.md#bhcm-fte).
